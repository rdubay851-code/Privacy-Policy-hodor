Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how HODOR, a Discord bot currently in development, may process information when you interact with the bot or use its features.

1. Overview

HODOR is an independent Discord bot and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Discord Inc. This policy describes the intended privacy approach for HODOR while it is in development.

By interacting with HODOR, you understand that information may be processed to provide requested bot features. Server owners should make sure their members understand the bot's role and the data its enabled features may process.

2. Information processed

Depending on which features are enabled and how the bot is configured, HODOR may process the following categories of information:

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and message IDs needed to identify users and perform commands.
  • Server and moderation information: moderation actions and reasons, warnings, mute/ban records, ticket information, and relevant audit or event logs.
  • Command and activity data: command inputs and settings, and—if the relevant tracking features are enabled—message or voice activity counts and related timestamps.
  • User-provided content: text or details you submit through commands, tickets, reports, or support interactions.
  • Technical diagnostics: error details and operational logs needed to troubleshoot and maintain the bot.

HODOR does not need your Discord password. Never share passwords, authentication tokens, or other login credentials with the bot or its maintainers.

Important: This list describes potential processing based on the bot's feature set. Only publish this policy after confirming it matches the features actually enabled and the data your deployment stores.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to commands and provide server-management features.
  • Apply moderation actions and communicate moderation notices where Discord allows delivery.
  • Maintain tickets, logs, configuration, leaderboards, and activity tracking when those features are enabled.
  • Protect the bot and servers against spam, abuse, or misuse.
  • Diagnose errors, maintain reliability, and improve features during development.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

HODOR should process information only for legitimate bot operations and should not sell personal information.

4. Storage and retention

Some features may require information to be stored in a database or logs so settings, tickets, moderation history, or statistics continue to work. Retention periods may vary by feature and by the server's configuration.

Because HODOR is in development, exact retention periods may not yet be standardized. Before public launch, the operator should document actual storage locations, retention periods, backup practices, and a process for handling deletion requests. Do not promise a specific deletion timeline unless it is implemented and can be met.

5. Sharing and third-party services

Information may be processed by Discord to deliver bot commands, messages, moderation actions, and other platform functionality. Hosting, database, monitoring, or other infrastructure providers may also process technical data as needed to operate HODOR.

Information may be disclosed when necessary to comply with law, address security incidents, enforce applicable terms, or protect users and the service. HODOR does not sell personal information. Any third-party provider used by the bot remains subject to that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

6. Permissions and user control

Server administrators choose whether to add HODOR and which permissions to grant it. Grant only the permissions needed for the features you intend to use. Removing the bot or disabling a feature can stop future processing associated with that feature, but may not automatically erase previously stored records.

Discord privacy settings may prevent HODOR from sending direct messages. The bot cannot bypass Discord's privacy controls. Where a DM cannot be delivered, moderation actions may still occur and a configured server log may report the delivery failure.

For questions about information associated with your Discord ID, contact us using the details below. We may need enough information to identify the relevant records and verify the request, and we will handle requests subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

7. Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to bot tokens, databases, and hosting infrastructure should be restricted to authorized maintainers.

If you believe you have found a security issue or exposed data, contact us through the support server or email below. Do not post private credentials or sensitive personal information in a public channel.

8. Age and eligibility

Use HODOR only if you are permitted to use Discord under Discord's applicable terms and age requirements in your location. HODOR is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe information has been submitted inappropriately, contact us so we can review it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as HODOR's commands, features, infrastructure, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a revised version is published. Continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy to the extent permitted by law.

Since HODOR is currently in development, features and data practices may change. Significant changes should be reflected here before or when they take effect.
